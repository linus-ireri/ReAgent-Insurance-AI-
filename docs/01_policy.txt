MAVUNO TEXTILES LIMITED
COMMERCIAL FIRE AND SPECIAL PERILS INSURANCE POLICY

Insurer: Amani General Insurance Company Limited
Policy Number: AGI/FIRE/2025/00842
Insured: Mavuno Textiles Limited
Risk Address: Plot 14, Kariobangi Industrial Area, Nairobi, Kenya
Occupancy/Use: Textile manufacturing (spinning, weaving, dyeing and garment finishing)
Period of Insurance: 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026

SECTION A: PROPERTY INSURED

Item 1 - Buildings (factory shed, dye house, warehouse, administration block)
  Sum Insured: KES 220,000,000

Item 2 - Plant and Machinery (spinning frames, looms, dyeing vats, boilers, generators)
  Sum Insured: KES 180,000,000

Item 3 - Stock (raw cotton, yarn, finished garments, packaging materials)
  Sum Insured: KES 90,000,000

Total Sum Insured: KES 490,000,000

SECTION B: PERILS COVERED

This Policy covers loss or damage to the Property Insured caused by:
  1. Fire
  2. Lightning
  3. Explosion (domestic boilers and gas only, unless extended)
  4. Aircraft and other aerial devices
  5. Riot, strike and malicious damage
  6. Storm, tempest, flood
  7. Impact by vehicles

SECTION C: EXCLUSIONS

This Policy does not cover:
  1. Loss or damage caused by the insured's own wilful act or gross negligence.
  2. Loss or damage arising from war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, civil war, or
     terrorism, unless specifically bought back by endorsement.
  3. Loss or damage where the Insured has failed to maintain the fire protection and
     safety measures declared at inception (see Warranty 1 below).
  4. Consequential loss, including loss of profits, business interruption, or loss of
     market, unless separately insured under a Business Interruption Policy.
  5. Loss or damage to electrical machinery caused by over-running, excessive pressure,
     short circuiting, self-heating or leakage of electricity, unless fire ensues, in
     which case only the ensuing fire damage is covered.
  6. Loss or damage occurring while the premises are left unoccupied for a continuous
     period exceeding 30 days, unless declared and agreed by the Insurer in writing.
  7. Fines, penalties, or punitive damages of any kind.
  8. Loss discovered more than 30 days after the Insured first became aware, or ought
     reasonably to have become aware, of circumstances giving rise to a claim, unless
     late notification is excused in writing by the Insurer.

SECTION D: WARRANTIES

Warranty 1 (Fire Protection Warranty): It is warranted that the Insured shall, at all
times during the currency of this Policy, maintain in good working order:
  (a) a functioning automatic sprinkler system covering the dye house and warehouse;
  (b) portable fire extinguishers serviced within the preceding 12 months, positioned
      at intervals not exceeding 30 metres throughout the factory floor;
  (c) a fire hydrant system connected to a water reserve of not less than 50,000 litres;
  (d) an internal fire brigade or trained fire marshal team of not fewer than 6 persons
      per shift.
Breach of this Warranty, if material to the cause or extent of the loss, entitles the
Insurer to decline or reduce indemnity in respect of the affected claim.

Warranty 2 (Housekeeping Warranty): It is warranted that flammable waste (cotton
lint, thread ends, packaging offcuts) shall be removed from production areas at the
end of each working shift and stored in designated fire-resistant bins located at
least 15 metres from the main production floor.

SECTION E: EXCESS/DEDUCTIBLE

Each and every claim: KES 2,500,000, or 2% of the claim amount, whichever is greater.

SECTION F: CONDITIONS PRECEDENT TO LIABILITY

  1. The Insured shall notify the Insurer within 14 days of any occurrence likely to
     give rise to a claim.
  2. The Insured shall furnish all information and documentary evidence reasonably
     required by the Insurer, including invoices, stock records, and maintenance
     records for fire protection equipment.
  3. The Insured shall take all reasonable steps to minimise or avert loss.
  4. No admission, offer, promise, or payment shall be made by or on behalf of the
     Insured without the written consent of the Insurer.
