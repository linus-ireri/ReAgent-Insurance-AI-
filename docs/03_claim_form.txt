1. DETAILS OF LOSS

Date of Loss: 3 August 2026
Time of Loss: Approximately 02:40 hrs
Date Reported to Insurer: 4 August 2026
Cause of Loss (as understood by Insured): Fire, believed to have started in the
dye house near the boiler room, spreading to the adjoining warehouse before being
brought under control by Nairobi County Fire Brigade at approximately 06:15 hrs.

2. DESCRIPTION OF INCIDENT

The night shift supervisor, Mr. Peter Kamau, reported smelling smoke at
approximately 02:35 hrs and raised the alarm. The internal fire marshal team
attempted to contain the fire using portable extinguishers but were unable to
control it due to its rapid spread through stored cotton lint in the warehouse
section adjoining the dye house. The Nairobi County Fire Brigade was called at
02:48 hrs and arrived on site at approximately 03:20 hrs. The fire was fully
extinguished by 06:15 hrs. No fatalities were reported. Two employees were treated
for smoke inhalation at Kenyatta National Hospital and discharged the same day.

3. ITEMS/PROPERTY AFFECTED

  - Dye house structure and dyeing vats: substantially destroyed
  - Adjoining warehouse structure: significant fire and water damage
  - Stock (raw cotton, yarn, packaging materials) stored in warehouse: total loss
  - Finished garment stock in a separate, unaffected store: no damage reported
  - Administration block: minor smoke damage only
  - Spinning and weaving floor (separate building): no fire damage reported, minor
    smoke residue reported by the Insured, still being assessed
