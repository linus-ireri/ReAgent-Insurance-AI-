KENYA REINSURANCE CORPORATION LIMITED
FIRE AND SPECIAL PERILS SURPLUS REINSURANCE TREATY

Treaty Reference: KR/AGI/SUR/2026
Reinsurer: Kenya Reinsurance Corporation Limited ("Kenya Re")
Cedant: Amani General Insurance Company Limited ("the Cedant")
Class of Business: Fire and Special Perils (Commercial and Industrial risks)
Treaty Type: Surplus (proportional)
Period: 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026, and each anniversary thereafter unless
terminated in accordance with Article 14.

ARTICLE 1 - SCOPE

This Treaty covers the Cedant's Fire and Special Perils portfolio written in the
Republic of Kenya, including manufacturing, warehousing, commercial and industrial
occupancies, subject to the terms, conditions, limits and exclusions set out below.

ARTICLE 2 - CEDANT'S RETENTION AND TREATY CAPACITY

2.1 The Cedant's retention (Line) for any one risk is KES 50,000,000 of Sum Insured.
2.2 The Treaty provides 8 Lines of automatic surplus capacity, being KES 400,000,000
    in excess of the Cedant's retention, giving total automatic capacity of
    KES 450,000,000 per risk.
2.3 Kenya Re participates on each risk ceded in the same proportion as the surplus
    amount bears to the total Sum Insured of that risk (the "Cession Percentage").
2.4 Kenya Re's share of any claim is calculated by applying the Cession Percentage
    to the amount of the claim payable by the Cedant under the original Policy,
    after deduction of the Policy excess.

ARTICLE 3 - RISKS REQUIRING ENHANCED ASSESSMENT

3.1 Any risk with a total Sum Insured exceeding KES 300,000,000 shall be classified
    as an Enhanced Assessment Risk.
3.2 For Enhanced Assessment Risks, the Cedant shall, prior to binding cover, provide
    Kenya Re with: (a) a detailed risk survey report not older than 24 months;
    (b) confirmation of fire protection measures in place; (c) loss history for the
    preceding 5 years; and (d) construction and occupancy details.
3.3 Underwriting guideline reference: Section 4.2 of the Kenya Re Underwriting
    Manual requires that Enhanced Assessment Risks be reviewed by a Senior
    Underwriter before automatic cession is confirmed, and that any risk with
    Sum Insured exceeding KES 400,000,000 be referred for facultative
    consideration outside this Treaty.

ARTICLE 4 - EXCLUSIONS UNDER THE TREATY

This Treaty excludes and Kenya Re shall have no liability in respect of:
  4.1 War, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities, civil war, rebellion,
      insurrection, or terrorism.
  4.2 Nuclear risks of any kind.
  4.3 Losses arising from the Cedant's failure to comply with a Warranty under the
      original Policy, where such failure is material to the loss.
  4.4 Losses where the original Policy excess has not first been applied.
  4.5 Business interruption or consequential loss, unless separately agreed by
      addendum to this Treaty.
  4.6 Any risk bound by the Cedant in breach of the underwriting guidelines
      referenced in Article 3.3.

ARTICLE 5 - CLAIMS NOTIFICATION AND COOPERATION

5.1 The Cedant shall notify Kenya Re within 14 days of becoming aware of any claim
    or circumstance likely to involve this Treaty, and in any event immediately for
    any claim where the estimated gross loss exceeds KES 20,000,000.
5.2 The Cedant shall provide Kenya Re with all documentation reasonably required to
    assess the claim, including the claim form, original Policy, loss adjuster or
    investigation reports, invoices, photographs, and any other supporting evidence.
5.3 Kenya Re shall be entitled to appoint its own loss adjuster, at its own cost, to
    review any claim where the Cedant's estimated gross loss exceeds
    KES 50,000,000, or where Kenya Re reasonably considers the claim to be unusual
    having regard to the Cedant's historical claims experience under this Treaty.
5.4 Claims shall be settled by the Cedant in accordance with the terms of the
    original Policy. Kenya Re shall follow the fortunes of the Cedant, provided
    that the claim has been handled by the Cedant in good faith and in accordance
    with the terms of the original Policy and this Treaty.

ARTICLE 6 - CLAIMS REQUIRING PRIOR REFERRAL TO KENYA RE

6.1 Notwithstanding Article 5.4, the Cedant shall obtain Kenya Re's prior written
    agreement before settling any claim where:
    (a) the estimated gross loss exceeds KES 60,000,000; or
    (b) there is a reasonable suspicion of arson, fraud, or material
        misrepresentation at inception; or
    (c) the cause of loss, or the applicability of a Policy exclusion or
        Warranty, is genuinely in doubt; or
    (d) required documentation under the original Policy (including but not
        limited to fire investigation or loss adjuster reports) has not yet
        been furnished.
6.2 Where none of the conditions in Article 6.1 apply, and the claim is otherwise
    consistent with the Cedant's normal claims experience, the Cedant may proceed
    to settle the claim without further reference to Kenya Re, subject to
    Article 5.2.

ARTICLE 7 - PREMIUM AND COMMISSION

Kenya Re shall receive its Cession Percentage of the original gross premium, less
a ceding commission of 30%, payable quarterly.

ARTICLE 8 - CURRENCY

All amounts under this Treaty are expressed in Kenya Shillings (KES) unless
otherwise stated.

ARTICLE 9 - CATASTROPHE PROTECTION

This Treaty operates net of the Cedant's own Catastrophe Excess of Loss
protection, where applicable. A single occurrence affecting multiple risks ceded
under this Treaty shall be aggregated for the purposes of any such protection.

ARTICLE 14 - DURATION AND TERMINATION

This Treaty renews annually unless either party gives not less than 3 months'
written notice prior to any anniversary date.

Executed between the Cedant and Kenya Reinsurance Corporation Limited.
