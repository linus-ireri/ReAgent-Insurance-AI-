INDEPENDENT FIRE INVESTIGATION REPORT

Prepared by: Ridgeway Loss Adjusters & Assessors Ltd
Report Reference: RWA/FIRE/2026/0341
For: Amani General Insurance Company Limited
Re: Mavuno Textiles Limited - Plot 14, Kariobangi Industrial Area, Nairobi
Claim Reference: AGI/CLM/2026/1187
Date of Report: 20 August 2026
Date of Site Inspection: 8 August 2026

1. INSTRUCTIONS

We were instructed by Amani General Insurance Company Limited to attend the site,
investigate the cause and origin of the fire of 3 August 2026, and to comment on
the extent of loss and the condition of fire protection systems at the time of the
incident.

2. FINDINGS - CAUSE AND ORIGIN

2.1 Physical examination of the fire scene indicates the fire originated in the
    dye house, in the vicinity of Dyeing Vat No. 3, close to an electrical
    distribution panel serving the dye house boiler.
2.2 Char patterns and burn depth analysis are consistent with an electrical fault
    at or near the distribution panel as the most probable ignition source. We
    found no physical evidence of accelerant use, forced entry, or any indicator
    consistent with deliberate ignition.
2.3 The fire then spread rapidly into the adjoining warehouse through an open
    connecting doorway. The warehouse contained loosely stored cotton lint and
    packaging materials, which are highly combustible and contributed materially
    to the speed of fire spread.
2.4 In our professional opinion, the most probable cause of the fire is accidental,
    arising from an electrical fault, with rapid spread aided by combustible
    material storage practices in the warehouse. We are unable to entirely rule
    out alternative causes given the extent of destruction in the immediate
    vicinity of the point of origin, but no evidence was found to support any
    cause other than accidental electrical fault.

3. FINDINGS - FIRE PROTECTION SYSTEMS

3.1 Sprinkler System: We requested maintenance and inspection records for the
    automatic sprinkler system covering the dye house and warehouse, as required
    under Warranty 1(a) of the Policy. At the time of our inspection, the Insured
    was unable to produce a maintenance record for the preceding 9 months. Visual
    inspection of sprinkler heads recovered from the debris in the dye house
    showed at least 3 heads that appeared to have been painted over, which can
    impair activation. We recommend the Insurer request the full maintenance
    history directly from the service contractor named by the Insured
    (Safeguard Fire Systems Ltd) to establish whether the system was in good
    working order at the time of loss.
3.2 Portable Extinguishers: Service tags confirmed extinguishers were serviced in
    March 2026, consistent with the Insured's declaration and within policy
    requirements.
3.3 Housekeeping: We noted that cotton lint and packaging offcuts were stored in
    open areas of the warehouse rather than in the fire-resistant bins specified
    under Warranty 2 of the Policy, and were located significantly closer to the
    production floor than the 15-metre separation specified. This is a probable
    contributing factor to the speed and extent of fire spread, though it did not,
    in our assessment, cause the fire's ignition.
3.4 Fire Hydrant System: Found to be in working order and was used by the Nairobi
    County Fire Brigade during firefighting operations.

4. ESTIMATED EXTENT OF LOSS (Adjuster's Preliminary Assessment)

  Buildings (dye house - total loss; warehouse - substantial damage):
      Adjuster estimate:                          KES 34,500,000
  Plant and Machinery (dyeing vats, boiler, ancillary equipment):
      Adjuster estimate:                          KES 19,800,000
  Stock (raw cotton, yarn, packaging - warehouse contents):
      Adjuster estimate, pending final stock reconciliation:
                                                    KES 21,000,000 to 24,000,000
  Smoke damage to administration block:
      Adjuster estimate:                           KES 650,000
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Preliminary Adjuster Estimate (Total):    KES 76,000,000 to 79,000,000

This preliminary estimate is below the Insured's own claimed figure of
KES 85,000,000, principally due to differences in the assessed value of destroyed
stock, which remains subject to final reconciliation against stock records once
the August ledger entries are made available.

5. OBSERVATIONS RELEVANT TO POLICY WARRANTIES

We note for the Insurer's consideration that the apparent lapse in sprinkler
system maintenance (Warranty 1(a)) and the housekeeping practices observed
(Warranty 2) may be relevant to the question of warranty compliance. We are not
qualified to opine on the legal effect of any breach; this is a matter for the
Insurer's claims and legal assessment. We can confirm, however, that the fire
protection deficiencies noted above are, in our professional opinion, more likely
than not to have contributed to the extent of the loss, even though they are not
assessed as the cause of ignition.

6. RECOMMENDATIONS

  1. Obtain the full sprinkler maintenance history from Safeguard Fire Systems Ltd
     directly.
  2. Obtain finalised stock ledger entries for August 2026 to confirm stock loss.
  3. Obtain invoices or asset register entries for machinery claimed as a total
     loss to substantiate the claimed value.
  4. We consider the claim, subject to the above outstanding items and the
     Insurer's own assessment of warranty compliance, to be a genuine loss
     arising from an accidental cause.
